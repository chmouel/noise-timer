Noise Timer is a simple, private companion for sleep, focus, and quiet moments.

Choose white, pink, or brown noise, adjust the volume, and let the sound play in the background. Set a sleep timer from a quick preset or choose your own duration. An optional fade-out gently lowers the volume during the final 20 seconds.

Features:

• White, pink, and brown noise generated in real time
• Adjustable playback volume
• Sleep timer with presets and custom durations
• Optional fade-out before the timer ends
• Background playback controls in the notification
• Remembers your preferred settings

Noise Timer has no ads, accounts, trackers, or network access.
